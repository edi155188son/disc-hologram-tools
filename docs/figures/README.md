# Figures

All figures here are **simulations**. The corresponding designs were burned on real BD-R discs
(Pioneer BDR-S12JX, stock firmware, Verbatim BD-R, ordinary ISO images) and the predicted
behaviour was confirmed **by eye only**. Calibrated photographs and instrument measurements are
not available yet and will be added when the quantitative test setup is ready.

| File | What it shows | Real-disc status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bd_dp_head_rb_sim.png` | Double-phase radial-carrier head hologram (rainbow/HPO block, avatar_bd2 v2), central 4 mm, 532 nm, three horizontal view angles (−6°, 0°, +6°), simulated on the BD byte grid with 15 % uncontrollable bytes. Last panel: mean reflectance (uniform, i.e. no zero-order "photograph"). | Burned; the head showed horizontal parallax in green and white light (visual). |
| `bd_dp_ksweep_sim.png` | Simulated signal / encoding-noise ratio in the +1 window versus carrier period k (tracks per period) for double-phase encoding on the real BD byte layout. Dips near k ≈ 8 and k ≈ 12 ("forbidden zones"). | Consistent with the block ranking on the colour test discs (the only block with all channels outside the dips performed best); not yet measured directly. |
